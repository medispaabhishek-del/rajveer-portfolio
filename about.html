How it started

Five years ago I was editing videos as a hobby, helping a friend cut his YouTube uploads on weekends. Over time I fell in love with storytelling through editing, sound design and color grading, and I turned that love into a full-time career.

What makes me different

I don't just cut clips together. I build a narrative that keeps the viewer watching until the very end: a hook that earns attention, pacing that respects it, and sound and color that make every scene feel intentional.

My mission

To help creators and brands turn raw, unorganized footage into polished stories that grow their audience.
